FIELD MANUAL Controls / moves / comfort

Your keyboard. Your problem.

Click any binding, then press a key to remap it. Changes are saved only on this device. Esc and Tab stay available.

Read the room. Then hit it.

  • Light → heavy: confirm a light hit, then tap heavy for a two-hit combo.
  • Down + heavy: sweep beneath standing guard. Down + block guards low.
  • Throw: beats close guarding. Jump or back away to escape.
  • Special: Ledger has reach; Clinker punishes jumping opponents.
  • Psyche-out: costs 50 ego. A long, interruptible taunt stuns in range. Block, jump clear or hit the windup. Ledger also drains 20 ego.
  • No chip damage. A held attack never repeats. Tap again. Pausing clears held keys.

Sharing a keyboard can cause hardware ghosting. Remap conflicting keys. CPU is a simple sparring partner.
